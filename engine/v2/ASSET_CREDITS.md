# 3D character asset credits

SOLO Engine V2 uses the following third-party assets, bundled into the GitHub Pages build:

- Male Soldier 01, 02, 03 and 04
- Female Soldier 01 and 02
- Shared Biped animation clips: idle, walk and run

Source: [CodeLove — Rigged Professions](https://models.codelove.in/models/collection/rigged-professions/) and [CodeLove — Character Animations](https://models.codelove.in/animations/).

License: MIT. The source pages state that each GLB includes its license metadata and that the complete packs include the license file. The assets are downloaded by the Pages workflow into the deployment artifact; they are not hand-built primitive meshes.

Character models are rigged humanoid GLB assets with 80-bone Biped skeletons, PBR materials and embedded textures. The six selected soldiers are approximately 1.78–1.85 m tall and range from about 12,957 to 15,517 triangles each.

The models and animation clips remain third-party assets. The game code does not claim authorship of them.
